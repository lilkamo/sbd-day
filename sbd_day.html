The one rule
Nothing above 85% today. One day rest before 1RM Monday. The goal is to feel the weight, move well, and walk out confident — not to PR.
Session rules
📋
Order: squat → bench → deadlift. Competition order. CNS is freshest for squats, deadlift goes last.
🎯
One top single at 85% per lift. If it moves well and feels easy — do not add more weight. That feeling is exactly what you want going into Monday.
🔄
Back-offs at 75% of 1RM. Two sets of 3 on squat and bench. One set of 3 on deadlift only — then session is done.
⏱
Rest times. 3–4 min after squat/bench top set. 4–5 min after deadlift top set. 2–3 min between back-offs.
🚫
No accessories. No cardio. Session ends when the deadlift back-off is done.
Recovery before Monday
🍽
Eat at maintenance today and Sunday. Not at deficit. You need glycogen in the tank for Monday's attempts.
💧
3.5L water both days. Dehydration kills strength output.
😴
8+ hours sleep Saturday and Sunday night. Most important recovery variable. Non-negotiable.
🚶
Sunday is full rest. Walk only — no training, nothing that taxes the CNS.
Monday 1RM mindset
🔁
Warm up exactly the same way as today. Same ladder, same feel. Don't change anything that works.
🧠
Open conservatively at 90–92%. The second attempt is where you push. Don't blow it on the opener.
⬆️
Jump 5–7.5% between attempts if the first goes well. Chase the lift, not a number.
