Session rules
Order: squat → bench → deadlift. Competition order. CNS is freshest for squats, deadlift goes last.
One top single per lift. If it moves well and feels easy — do not add weight. That feeling is the point.
Back-off sets at 10% below top. Two sets of 3 on squat and bench. One set only on deadlift.
Rest 3–4 min after top sets. 2–3 min between back-offs. 4–5 min after deadlift top set.
No accessories. No cardio. Session ends when the deadlift back-off is done.
